Privacy Policy

Flight of Icarus · Effective September 8, 2026

Flight of Icarus is a single-player game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

Information we collect

None. The app does not gather personal data of any kind, including name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes:

  • Highest altitude reached and collected light totals
  • Which Olympus upgrades you have purchased
  • Whether you have already heard the narrated story

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no social media integrations. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Network access

The app does not require an internet connection and does not send data over the network during normal play.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces features that handle data differently — for example leaderboards or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to everymansodyssey@outlook.com.
